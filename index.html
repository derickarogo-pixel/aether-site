What it does

  • Checks the daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, 15-minute and 5-minute charts.
  • Alerts you only when most timeframes agree, then waits for a confirmation candle.
  • Warns you early when a setup is forming, and holds back during unusually volatile markets.
  • Records every signal and tells you when it reaches a target or its stop loss.

Where it stands

Aether is experimental. We tested the rules on several months of gold data and the results were mixed, which does not predict future performance. Practise on a demo account before risking real money.

This is not financial advice. Trading gold carries a high risk of loss, and a position that is too large can lose more than you expected. The bot never places trades for you. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
